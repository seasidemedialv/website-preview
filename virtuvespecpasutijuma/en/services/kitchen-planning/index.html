Kitchen planning for comfortable everyday life

A functional kitchen layout determines whether the kitchen will be comfortable on a daily basis. It is important not to follow one universal scheme, but to adjust the space according to human habits.

Practical guide

Logic of work areas, storage and movement

The kitchen arrangement is good if the most frequent activities form an understandable sequence. Placing, washing, preparing and cooking products should not require excessive movement or crossing with open doors. A universal "work triangle" is only a starting principle, not a ready-made answer for every room.

Kitchen planning also includes what is not visible at first glance: where the pots will be stored, where the shopping bags will be placed, how many people will be using the worktop at the same time, and whether an open dishwasher can be passed. A kitchen plan and kitchen layout becomes convincing when these scenarios are tested and not just drawn.

Scenario Testing

Play an evening in the new kitchen

Imagine unloading groceries, washing produce, cooking and picking up dishes. Where will you put the bags? Can you pass an open dishwasher? Are the pots near the cooking area and the everyday dishes near the wash?

Plan for two people

If two people are working in the kitchen at the same time, their main routes should not constantly cross. Such a check often shows more than an abstract module diagram and helps to change the sequence of zones in time.

Before consultation

How to prepare useful starting information

Write down a typical cooking sequence and items that must have a place. Specify the number of people, the appliances, the need for a place to eat, and what causes inconvenience in the existing kitchen. This information helps to base the kitchen layout on real scenarios and not just a row of modules.
