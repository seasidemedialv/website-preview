Practical guide

An island with a specific function and sufficient space for movement

A kitchen island is justified when it has a clear task: additional work surface, storage, seating or a combination of several functions. Aisles, drawer openings and movement between the island and the main line of the kitchen should be checked before choosing a shape.

The island changes the center of the room, so not only the furniture itself should be evaluated, but also the view from the living area, the position of the lights and the movement of people around it. If appliances or a sink are planned on the island, the necessary technical solutions must be coordinated with the relevant specialists before production.

An island or peninsula

When is a peninsula a more practical choice?

A kitchen with a peninsula at one end connects to a wall or main line of furniture. It can provide additional work surface, storage or seating, while not requiring clear passage on all sides. Such a solution may be more suitable for a room where a full island would reduce freedom of movement.

Comparing the options, we check the main direction of movement, the opening of drawers and appliances, the use of seats and the connection with the rest of the kitchen line. The peninsula is not a smaller version of the island - it creates a different flow of the room and visually separates the kitchen area more clearly.

Selection Principle

The island is accessible from several sides and becomes the center of the room. The peninsula retains one anchor point and can use the available space more economically. In both cases, the main thing is sufficient movement space, not the name of the furniture.

Important nuances

Three mistakes that can weaken the final result

Choose too big island

A larger surface isn't a benefit if it becomes awkward to move around and open cabinets.

Do not choose the main function

If an island tries to do too many tasks at once, each of them may run out of space.

Forget the view from the living room

In an open space, the side of the island, the back, and everyday clutter are part of the interior.

Before consultation

How to prepare useful starting information

Specify the overall dimensions of the room, the main directions of movement and the desired function of the island. If seating is required, add the number of people;if the technique - the specific models or gauges. This allows you to understand in time whether the frost really gives the room a functional benefit.
