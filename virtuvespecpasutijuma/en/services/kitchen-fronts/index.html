Practical guide

Appearance, proportions and maintenance of fronts in one decision

Kitchen fronts occupy a large part of the visible space, therefore their tone, texture and division affect the entire interior. However, the facade is also a surface that is often touched on a daily basis. We judge the choice by grip, care habits, light and construction, not just by a small sample.

One type of facade material or finish can have different visual effects depending on the area and direction. A dark tone on the wall of the tall cabinets works differently than on one of the lower rows, but a pronounced texture requires deliberate blending and coordination with the work surface.
