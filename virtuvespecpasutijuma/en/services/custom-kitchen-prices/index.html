Practical guide

Clear offer showing kitchen size and options

For the question "how much does a custom kitchen cost?"there is no fair one-size-fits-all answer without room and kit. Kitchens of the same length can differ significantly in terms of the number of cabinets, the proportion of drawers, high modules, division of fronts, hardware and complexity of installation.

Built-in kitchen prices should be evaluated together with the content of the offer. A cheaper summary does not necessarily include the same amount of delivery, assembly, work surface or machinery. Our goal is to first find out the needs and then prepare a comparable volume - without a fictitious price, before the essential parameters are known.

Three mistakes that can weaken the final result

Ask for price per meter

This figure does not show corners, drawers, tall cabinets or hardware.

Do not submit hardware list

The dimensions of the equipment affect the modules and sometimes also the work surface solution.
