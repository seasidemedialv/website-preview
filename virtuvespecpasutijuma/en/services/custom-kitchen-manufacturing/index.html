Practical guide

Manufacturing after approved design and agreed specification

Kitchen manufacturing begins only when project dimensions, modules, division of fronts and principles of hardware are mutually agreed upon. At the production stage, an unclear decision becomes a detail, therefore before it, contradictions between the visual design, the dimensions of the equipment and the actual conditions of the room must be eliminated.

The manufacture of custom kitchen cabinetry is not separated from measurements and installation. The sequence of parts, edge treatment, attachment points and assembly all affect how the modules can be brought into the room, assembled and adjusted. The production price, on the other hand, is to be assessed by volume and specification, not by one running meter.

From drawing to kit

Checkpoints during kitchen construction

  1. Project version. Only an agreed drawing and specification are used for manufacture.
  2. Part compatibility. Dimensions, fasteners and mechanisms of fronts are checked against the modular construction.
  3. Marked set. Prepared parts must be associated with their place in the project.
  4. Assembly sequence. Access to the room and the order of connecting the modules should be taken into account before delivery.

Important nuances

Three mistakes that can weaken the final result

Change technique too late

Even a small difference in dimensions can affect the niche, ventilation and division of the facade.

Compare only the final amount

Offers are not comparable if the volume of modules, hardware, delivery and installation is not the same.

Do not leave assembly tolerances

The room is not a perfect rectangle; the design must take into account the actual wall and floor conditions.
