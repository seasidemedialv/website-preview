Practical guide

Cabinets whose interior contents determine exterior size

Kitchen cabinets are useful when each module already has an understandable content in the project. Pots, plates, food, small appliances and waste sorting require different heights, depths and access. Therefore, we do not create a row of cabinets based only on the symmetry of the fronts.

Hanging kitchen cabinets help to use the wall, but their height should be related to reach, work surface and room proportions. Kitchen corner cabinets, on the other hand, should be evaluated according to access, not just the total volume - a deep area is not practical if its contents are not visible on a daily basis.

Important nuances

Three mistakes that can weaken the final result

Count only shelves

Storage quality is also determined by visibility, grip and the path of the case to the workplace.

Forget gravity

Heavy stuff needs a suitable area and mechanism, not just free size.

Split fronts without content

Many narrow cabinets can create unnecessary partitions and a fragmented view.

Before consultation

How to prepare useful starting information

Group kitchen contents: everyday dishes, pots, food, small appliances, cleaning and sorting. Mark the bulkiest items and their frequency of use. Such a list helps to base the dimensions and internal organization of the cabinets on the basis of need.
