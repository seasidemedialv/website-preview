Practical guide

Mechanisms selected by module load and frequency of use

Kitchen hardware are hinges, guides, lifting and internal organization mechanisms that determine the daily operation of the furniture. They cannot be meaningfully selected only by name: you need to know the size of the facade, the load of the module, the direction of opening and how often the particular place will be used.

A well-chosen mechanism does not solve a bad design. Before the hardware, it should be determined whether the contents will be more visible in a shelf, drawer or pull-out system. The kitchen hardware for the dish dryer should also be related to the size of the cabinet, ventilation, water drainage and easy human reach.
