Practical guide

The kitchen as a coordinated room composition

The interior of the kitchen connects the furniture with the floor, walls, light and adjacent rooms. Even a functionally good plan can look jerky if elevation lines, material transitions, and visible technical elements are not considered together. Therefore, the interior of the kitchen should be designed from the overview to the details.

Kitchen interior colors change depending on daylight, lighting temperature and adjacent surfaces. The impression of a designer kitchen is often not created by an expensive accent, but by consistency: repeated lines, balanced areas and a clear hierarchy of materials.

Important nuances

Three mistakes that can weaken the final result

Choose color by phone

Screen and photos do not show the actual tone and surface response to light.

Copy everything from one reference

The space, light and proportions shown in the picture are not repeated in your home.

Forget everyday objects

The coffee machine, tableware and textiles also take part in the interior, so they need a conscious place.

Before consultation

How to prepare useful starting information

Take pictures of the room at different times of the day and add samples or pictures of the decoration next to it. Complete the references with an explanation of what you like about them. It helps to design the interior of the kitchen for your space, not an abstract visualization.
