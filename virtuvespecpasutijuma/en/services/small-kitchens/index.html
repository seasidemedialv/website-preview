Practical guide

Compact kitchen with clearly defined priorities

A small kitchen requires more choices, not less design. In a limited space, every change in depth, opening and work surface affects the rest of the plan. Therefore, we first determine the mandatory equipment, the most frequently used items and the minimum required free surface.

In a small kitchen, height can be used for storage, but not everything needs to be on the top shelf. Everyday content should be placed in the reachable area, and less frequently used content should be placed higher. Visual peace is created not by emptiness, but by a disciplined division of fronts, deliberate colors and less fragmented lines.
