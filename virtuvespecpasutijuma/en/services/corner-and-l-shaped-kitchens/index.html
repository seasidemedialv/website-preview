Practical guide

Corner use without lost access and disturbing openings

Corner kitchen units allow you to connect two walls and create a compact work sequence, but the corner itself is not a storage benefit. It must be decided how to access it, how the work surface will continue and whether the adjacent drawers, handles and appliances will be able to open freely.

L-shaped kitchen appliances are suitable for rooms of different sizes, if both planes fulfill a clear function. One side can focus on tall cabinets and appliances, the other on preparation and washing. Ready-made corner custom kitchen cabinetry sets do not always respect the aisles, windowsills or communications of the specific room.

How to prepare useful starting information
