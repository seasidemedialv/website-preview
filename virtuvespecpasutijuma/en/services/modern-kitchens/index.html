Practical guide

Modern composition where visual peace rests in precise details

The character of a modern kitchen is not only made up of smooth fronts. Continuity of cabinet lines, well-thought-out placement of appliances, rhythm of handles or grips and a limited palette of materials are essential. The simpler the result looks, the more precisely its details must be coordinated.

Modern custom kitchen cabinetry can be warm, neutral or contrasting;"modern" is not a single color. In the long term, a solution that respects the light of the room, the finish of the adjacent floors and walls, and the overall character of the home, rather than blindly repeating a short-term visual effect, is more convincing.
