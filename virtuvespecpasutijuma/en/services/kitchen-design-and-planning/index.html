Practical guide

From everyday needs to a kitchen project to be made

Designing a kitchen begins with an understanding of space and people, not with a decorative image. The project should combine movement, work surfaces, storage, technical areas, lighting design and facade proportions. Only then does the visual language become part of a functional solution.

Kitchen design turns desires into verifiable decisions. The kitchen project helps to see if the openings do not conflict, the heights are comfortable and each module has a task. The visual design, on the other hand, should be evaluated in the real light of the room and in relation to the adjacent decoration, not in an isolated sample.

Project readability

What should a good kitchen design allow you to check?

Plan

Shows movement, module depths, tech slots and column relationships.

Facade views

Reveals splits, height lines, handle rhythm and visible side elements.

Spec

Name the surfaces to be matched, functions of mechanisms and open questions. Visualization helps to understand the mood, but does not replace the dimensions and the information necessary for production.

Important nuances

Three mistakes that can weaken the final result

Copy image without room analysis

References show mood, but not your bars, communications and movement restrictions.

Design only one view

Side views, openings and how the kitchen is seen from the adjacent room are also important.

Do not prioritize wishes

If all intentions are equally important, in the moment of conflict it is not clear what to preserve.

Before consultation

How to prepare useful starting information

Gather a plan of the room, photos in daylight, ideas for equipment and a few pictures where you can specifically name what you like. Also add daily habits and priorities. This allows the consultation to distinguish project requirements from visual preferences and to set priorities in time.
