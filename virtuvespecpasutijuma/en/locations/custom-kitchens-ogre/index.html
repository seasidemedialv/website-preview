Local context

What does kitchen project Ogre mean?

State city on the right bank of the Daugava at the mouth of the Ogre River, with differences in terrain and construction.

In Ogre, the terrain of the address and the position of the building can affect the delivery route, but the content of the kitchen is determined by the space itself. In the apartment project, we check the common areas and floor;in the house - how to get the modules from the driveway to the kitchen.

Address and delivery access

In a room with several windows or sloping passages, the amount of storage cannot be increased by covering important openings. First, we determine the role of the free walls, work areas and technical areas, then the composition of the fronts.

What to check at a property in Ogre

  • Describe the building type and note if the access has a slope, steps or different entry levels.
  • In the room, check the height of windows, door openings and directions of views that should not be blocked by tall cabinets.
  • Add floor, elevator and stair area dimensions to the apartment; for the house, indicate the gate and the way to the entrance.
  • In the case of renovation, record the connections to be preserved and finishing works that must be completed before accurate measurement.

What should be decided first in the Ogre project

Obscuring an ogre window or view with a tall cabinet unit can permanently degrade the quality of the room. The benefit of storage must be compared with daylight and the connection of the kitchen to the surroundings.

Local Risk Check

Ogre terrain and building type affect delivery

In the city of Ogre River and Daugava banks, addresses may be located in different terrain conditions. We don't turn this fact into a design cliché, but use it to check arrivals and level changes in time.

  • For the apartment project, we document the location of elevators, stairs and modules so that there are no surprises on the day of delivery.
  • In the house, we appreciate the path from the gate to the entrance and further to the kitchen, including outdoor and indoor steps.
  • We check the window and door openings of the room together with drawers, appliances and the depths of the work area.

We use the conclusions of the access and space inspection for the Ogre object in the evaluation of the project variants. Every significant limitation should be visible before coordinating fronts and materials so that the visual solution supports practical use.
