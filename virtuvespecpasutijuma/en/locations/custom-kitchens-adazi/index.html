What does a custom kitchen project in Ādaži involve?

The town of Pieriga on the left bank of the Gauja, which is divided into two parts by the Vējupe.

In Ādaži, the urban structure of water and roads means that the full address is essential for metering and delivery planning. In a kitchen project, the local context is not a decoration - it helps to understand the object's reach, while the furniture itself is determined by the data of the room.

Address and delivery access

In a house with a large common living area, kitchen fronts, island and high cabinets should also be evaluated from the adjacent room. In a more compact space, priority is given to a continuous work surface and transparent storage.

What should be checked at the Ādaži facility

  • Indicate in which part of Ādaži the property is located and whether the entrance can be accessed via a public road or a private driveway.
  • In an open plan, add placement of windows, patio doors, and dining areas so that cabinetry doesn't obscure important views.
  • Fix readiness of water, ventilation and electrical outlets;in new construction, mark which points are still changeable.
  • Before delivery, check the width of the gate, the cover, the external steps and a safe place to unload the modules.

Local Risk Check

Ādaži project check before drawing approval

Part of the address, Vējupe crossing and specific street access can change the survey and delivery route, but the kitchen design is determined by the space. Before approving the drawing, we separate the logistics of the place from the technical decisions of the furniture.

  • In an open plan, we check out how the tall cabinets and island look from the living area and entryway.
  • We leave the areas of windows, patio doors and heating elements usable even after opening all fronts.
  • For delivery, we fix gates, turns, entrance threshold and a safe place for temporary placement of modules.

We add this information to the starting data of the project, so that the measurement of the Adaž object, the inspection of the technical units and the subsequent assembly are parts of one sequential process. This way, the customer can see which issues need to be resolved before choosing materials and fronts.
