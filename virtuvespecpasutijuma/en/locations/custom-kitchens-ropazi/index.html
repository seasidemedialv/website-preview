What does the Ropaži kitchen project mean?

Lielciems in Ropaži county and the center of Ropaži parish in the eastern part of Pieriga.

In Ropaži, the full address is required for both room survey and delivery arrangements. The village and the wider parish include different addresses, so we do not use the place name as a substitute for information about building type or access.

Address and delivery access

In a private house, we plan the connection of the kitchen with the dining area and windows, but in an apartment or a compact house - opening conflicts and the continuity of the work surface. Before production, we check the actual dimensions regardless of the original plan.

What to check at a property in Ropaž

  • Specify the village, street, house number or name and parish so that the location of the object is not interpretable.
  • For a more remote address, describe the pavement, gate, width and turn-around option for the last leg of the road.
  • In the space of a private house, note the windows, the dining area and the main directions of movement through the common living space.
  • Before the final project, check the technical documentation, communication locations and dimensions of the finished finish.
