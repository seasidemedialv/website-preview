What does a custom kitchen project in Piņķi involve?

Babīte parish center in Pierīga at the intersection of A5 and A10 roads, on the right bank of Neriņa.

Piņķiis has both a denser center and a context of detached houses. In the project of an individual kitchen, this means that you cannot use one "Piņķi house" model - the architecture of a specific room, family habits and the readiness of construction work must be evaluated.

Address and delivery access

If the kitchen is in a new home, we check the final dimensions after finishing. If in an existing house, we fix the dismantling limit and the communications to be preserved. The gate, driveway, entrance door and turns to the room are important for delivery.

What to check at a property in Piņķii

  • Specify whether the property is a new or existing private house and at what stage the room is finished.
  • In the open living area, fix the windows, dining table, patio doors and visibility from the main seating areas.
  • Check communication end positions and equipment dimensions by models, not just by nominal width.
  • For delivery, specify the dimensions of the gate and entrance, driveway surface and turns to the kitchen room.

Decision Sequence

What should be decided first in the Piņķi project

In Piņķii new construction, visualization can help make room decisions, but it should not be confused with production-ready documentation. A final measurement is required between the two stages.

In an existing home, the first thing to do is determine what the current kitchen does poorly. Only after the list of functional problems, it makes sense to choose an external composition.

Local Risk Check

New and existing homes in Piņķi require different preparation

The most important risk in a new house is to fix the size too early;in an existing house - an untested assumption about the communications and wall planes to be preserved. In both cases, production requires the actual end state.

  • In new construction, we coordinate the moment of measurement with the completion of finishing and the choice of equipment.
  • In the existing kitchen, before the project, we determine the limit of dismantling, the connections to be preserved and the necessary room changes.
  • For the delivery of a private house, we check the gate, driveway, entrance height and turns to the open living area.

For the Piņķi object, these initial data help to compare the architecture of the space with daily habits and delivery options. Before approving the project, the customer can check each important assumption and understand how it affects the final solution.
