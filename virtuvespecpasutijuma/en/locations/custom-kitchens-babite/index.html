Site context

What does the Babīte kitchen project mean?

Lielciems, Mārupe county, Babītes parish, near the western border of Riga, near the railway and A5/A10 roads.

Babīte's historical road structure and building diversity mean that a specific address is more important than a general place name. The kitchen should be designed according to the room, and not according to the assumption of what kind of housing Babīte could have.

Address and delivery access

A site near railways or major roads may have easy access, but the last stage of delivery should always be checked separately. Provide details of gates, parking spaces, entrances and stairs where this may limit the movement of large modules.

What to check at a property in Babīte

  • Specify the full address in the application, because Babīte's name alone does not describe the specific delivery route.
  • For a private house, measure the gate, entrance and turns, and for an apartment, indicate the floor, elevator and communal doorways.
  • On the room plan, mark the windows, heating elements and existing connections to be preserved.
  • Add machinery gauges and door opening directions so they can be checked with aisle widths.

The facade material is not the first decision in the Babīte facility. First of all, you need to know whether the dimensions of the selected modules can be brought safely and whether the openings in the room do not conflict.

Local Risk Check

Babīte addresses and delivery scenario

The location of Babīte near the Riga border and the main transport routes does not cancel the inspection of the last stage of delivery. The historical road structure, the proximity of the railway and the entrance of the specific address should be evaluated separately from the kitchen plan.

  • If the house is on a narrower street, we check the unloading location before delivery and do not rely on the map image.
  • In the room near the outer wall, we check the windowsill, radiator, ventilation and whether the fronts can be opened completely.
  • In the plan, we reserve the work surface for everyday use, instead of automatically filling each free section of the wall with a high module.

The access information prepared for the Babīte object remains together with the room measurements and equipment list. It helps to distinguish design choices from technical limitations in time and not to make decisions that do not match the kitchen plan only based on a visual sample.
