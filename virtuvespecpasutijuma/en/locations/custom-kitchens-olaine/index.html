What does a custom kitchen project in Olaine involve?

The administrative center of Olaine county is approximately 20 kilometers from Riga, with a railway connection in the direction of Riga-Jelgava.

In the development of the city of Olaine, it is important to first determine the type of housing. In the kitchen of a multi-apartment building, compactness, dimensions of appliances and delivery through common areas must often be balanced;in a detached house - work areas and connection with the rest of the floor.

Address and delivery access

Before measuring, find out whether the wall decoration, floor or communications will change. For the delivery, indicate the floor, the size of the elevator and the possibility of placement, and for the project - the exact models of the equipment or at least the dimensions.

What to check in Olainein the object

  • Specify the housing type, floor and the presence of an elevator, since the starting data for an apartment and a private house are different.
  • In a compact kitchen, measure not only the walls, but also doorways, window sills, radiators and the free width of the aisle.
  • List the equipment you use every day and the contents you keep so you don't clutter the room with unnecessary modules.
  • Before delivery, check common corridors, stairwell turns and places where modules may be temporarily stored.

Olaine's compact kitchen priority list

In the kitchen of an apartment building, each module competes for limited space. First, we determine the mandatory equipment, free preparation area and daily storage;only after that we add less frequently used functions.

  • We make the deep lower cabinets transparent so that the storage volume is not only available on paper.
  • We check the openings of the refrigerator, sink and cooking area at the same time as the doors of the entrance and adjacent cabinets.
  • For delivery, we fix the turns of floors, elevators, stairs and where the modules can be placed without obstructing the common space.

In the Olaine project, specifying the building type allows you to ask the right questions already in the first consultation. As a result, the measurement preparation, the kitchen plan and the delivery route are viewed as related tasks rather than three separate steps.
