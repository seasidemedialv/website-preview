Site context

What does the Ulbroka kitchen project mean?

Administrative center of Ropaži county and Stopiņi parish at the intersection of P4 and P5 roads.

There are both multi-apartment and individual buildings in Ulbroka, so the kitchen layout cannot be based only on the city of the address. In the apartment, checking the compact space and delivery route is important;in the house - relation to adjacent areas and actual building dimensions.

Address and delivery access

The territory of Ulbroka has expanded over time, so please enter the full street and house number in the form. Before delivery, check the building entrance, stairs or elevator, as well as the place to safely place the modules.

What to check at the Ulbroka facility

  • Specify the building type in the application, as the Ulbroka apartment and private house require different access information.
  • For the apartment, fix the floor, the elevator, the turns of the stairs and the place for the temporary storage of the modules.
  • For a private house, check the relationship of the gate, entrance, window and patio door with the intended line of cabinets.
  • Regardless of building type, add equipment dimensions and exact measurements of finished space.

What should be decided first in the Ulbroka project

For an Ulbroka apartment, the biggest risk may be a narrow turn of the stairs, and for a private house, the conflict of the wardrobe line with the terrace door. Both cannot be solved with one standard checklist.

Determine which building scenario applies to a given address before approving the exterior design. This changes both the division of modules and the organization of works.

Local Risk Check

Both the address and the building type must be specified in Ulbroka

The expansion and diverse construction of the territory of Ulbroka means that the name of the city alone does not tell whether to plan the kitchen of an apartment or a private house. This distinction changes both the opening questions and the delivery preparation.

  • For the apartment, we gather floor, elevator and staircase information, but in the room we prioritize the work surface and safe openings.
  • For the house, we check the relationship of the gate, entrance, window and patio door to the cabinet lines.
  • The proximity of P4 and P5 roads helps to navigate, but we always check the last stage of delivery by the specific address.

In the Ulbroka project, the separation of building type helps to properly organize further questions about space, machinery and access. The customer can see which decisions are based on measurements and which still need to be clarified before production.
