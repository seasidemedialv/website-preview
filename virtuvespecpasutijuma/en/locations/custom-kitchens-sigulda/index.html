What does the Sigulda kitchen project mean?

Sigulda county town in the Gauja Senleja area with a distinct terrain and scattered access to various addresses.

In Sigulda, the slopes and terrain can affect the approach to a particular object, so before delivery you should know the gate, steps, entrance level and distance to the room. This cannot be reliably deduced from the name of the city alone.

Address and delivery access

In a house with large windows or a view of the landscape, we evaluate the high volumes of the kitchen so that they do not unduly block light and movement directions. In a more compact space, the focus shifts to work order and reachable storage.

What to check at a property in Sigulda

  • Fix the slope, outside steps and all levels between the landing area and the kitchen entrance.
  • In a room with large windows, note the directions of views and daylight that tall cabinets should not unreasonably obscure.
  • If the floor changes levels, measure at several points and indicate the transition location relative to the modules.
  • For delivery, measure the height of the door, the turns of the corridor and the place where the modules can be safely placed.

What should be decided first in Sigulda's project

In Sigulda, a wall of tall cabinets can provide plenty of storage, but a blocked window or view can be too high a price. Both benefits should be evaluated in the room plan.

Leveling should not be dealt with until assembly day. This can affect plinths, worktop connections and where the row of cabinets logically ends.

The terrain of Sigulda should be included in the delivery plan

A slope, stairs, or multiple levels between a driveway and an entrance can change the order in which modules are moved. We clarify this information before delivery, and do not deal with it only when the furniture is already in the facility.

  • We fix the distance to the entrance, outdoor steps, door height and internal turns to the kitchen room.
  • In a house with large windows, we group the high volumes in such a way as to preserve light and important view directions.
  • If the room has a change of levels, we base the work area and plinth solution on actual measurements at several points.
