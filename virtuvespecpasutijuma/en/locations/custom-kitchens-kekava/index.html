In the Ķekava project, the construction plan should be distinguished from the final room

If the home is still under construction, an architect or developer's plan is a good start, but not the final measurement of the build. Finishing, windowsills, corners and the actual places of communications can change the division of modules.

  • Before the final measurement, we complete the wall and floor layers that affect the geometry of the kitchen.
  • We approve the appliances models before fixing the niches and ventilation gaps, not after the furniture is made.
  • In the delivery description, we include the stage of the object, the gate, the entrance and whether other construction works will not interfere with the assembly.

At the Ķekava facility, such an inspection creates a common reference point for the customer and the designer. Once the address, building type, space and access have been specified, you can logically compare planning options and avoid random choices.
