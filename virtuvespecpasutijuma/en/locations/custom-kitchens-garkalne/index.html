What does the Garkalne kitchen project mean?

Settlement and parish territory in Ropaži district in the north-eastern direction of Pieriga.

The name of Garkalnes can refer to both a specific settlement and a wider area, therefore a full address is always required in the project application. We use the context of the place to prepare logistics, not typical kitchen assumptions.

Address and delivery access

In the project of a private house, the connection of the room with the dining area, windows and exit to the outside is important. The driveway, gate, entrance height and where the modules can be unloaded without damaging the finish must be approved prior to delivery.

What to check at a property in Garkalne

  • In the application, write the full address and parish, so that the Garkalne place name is not interpreted too broadly.
  • In a private driveway, check gate width, coverage, tree branches and the ability for vehicles to turn safely.
  • For the kitchen space, fix the dimensions of the end walls, windows and connections, without using the approximate data of the map or construction project.
  • List the distance from the drop-off point to the entrance and any steps or tight turns along the way.

Specifying the address of Garkalne is not a formality

The name of Garkalne in a conversation can mean a specific settlement or a wider area of ​​the parish. The full address eliminates the false assumption about the object's reach and allows measurement and delivery to be organized according to the actual route.

  • In a private house, we check the width of the gate, the canopy, the external steps and the opening direction of the entrance door.
  • In the kitchen space, we fix the windows, inclined transitions, heating and exit to the outside before choosing the cabinet heights.
  • If the construction works continue, we perform the final measurement only after the finishing layers and communication areas have stabilized.

In Garkalne's application, we record the full location of the object and the nature of the access in order to understand the scope of the work in the same way. Such an initial check allows the project conversation to focus on the function of the space, rather than correcting untested logistical assumptions later.
