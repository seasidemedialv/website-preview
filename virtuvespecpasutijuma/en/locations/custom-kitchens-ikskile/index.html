What does a custom kitchen project in Ikšķile involve?

Ogre county town on the right bank of Daugava between Riga and Ogre directions.

Ikšķile kitchen design can be found both in an existing home and in the latest construction of private houses. In both cases, the main thing is not to turn the local context into an assumption: the design is determined by the measurements of the space, the dimensions of the equipment and the habits of the users.

Address and delivery access

In a house with a view or an exit to the outside, we evaluate the position of the high cabinets and the island against the windows and the movement path. In the existing room, on the other hand, we clarify in time which communications remain and what is the readiness of the walls for assembly.

What to check at a property in Ikškile

  • Note the slope, exterior steps, and level change between the driveway and the house entrance.
  • Fix window and exterior door openings in the room so that the work area does not block light or the main path of movement.
  • In the renovation, specify which connections should be preserved and whether the wall finishing will be completed before the final measurement.
  • Add machinery models or gauges, especially for machines with doors that open to an aisle or an adjacent module.

Decision Sequence

What should be decided first in the Ikškile project

In Ikšilė, a large window or an exit to the outside can be the main quality of the room, so tall cabinets should not be placed only according to the amount of storage. Light and safe movement must be maintained.

Preserving existing connections can save rebuilding, but it shouldn't create an inconvenient work sequence. Both options should be compared before the modules are approved.

Local Risk Check

Ikšilė kitchen connection with light and outdoor space

In houses in the city facing the Daugava, windows, balcony or terrace exits can be an important part of the room composition. We do not replace them with the volume of cabinets just to increase the nominal storage space.

  • We group tall modules where they do not interrupt the path of natural light and the main line of movement.
  • If keeping existing communications, we check that they support the new work order instead of forcing an awkward plan.
  • We estimate gates, outdoor level changes, entrance turns and a place to protect the work area in the way of delivery.

For the Ikšķile object, terrain and entrance data are evaluated together with kitchen dimensions. Thus, planning, module division and delivery preparation are based on one verified set of information, which the customer can supplement before the final measurement.
