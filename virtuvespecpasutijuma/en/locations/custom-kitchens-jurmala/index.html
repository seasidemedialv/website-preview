What does a custom kitchen project in Jūrmala involve?

The state city between the Gulf of Riga and Lielupi, which consists of a series of different neighborhoods and where wooden architecture is important.

In Jūrmala, the name of the city is not enough - Ķemeri, Kauguri, Dubulti, Majori, Dzintari, Bulduri and other neighborhoods differ in their location and construction. The full address helps to plan the delivery, but the status of the building, the construction and the actual space determine how kitchen attachments can be handled.

Address and delivery access

A historic or wooden building should not automatically assume straight planes or free mounting space. On the other hand, in a seasonally used home, the intensity of use and storage needs should be discussed, without making untested conclusions about humidity or construction.

What to check at a property in Jūrmala

  • Specify the Jūrmala area, full address and type of building structure, not limited to the name of the city.
  • In a wooden or older building, check the straightness of the walls, the level of the floor and a secure base of attachment.
  • Describe whether the home is used year-round or seasonally, and how many people the kitchen serves at peak times.
  • Fix gate, yard cover, outside steps, narrow door and room distance for delivery.
