What does the Carnikava kitchen project mean?

A town in Ādaži county in the vicinity of the lower Gauja river and the coast of the Gulf of Riga with a railway connection.

In Carnikava, the exact address in the project application helps to separate the city center from the surrounding buildings and prepare the delivery. Proximity to the coast in itself does not give the right to draw conclusions about the construction of a specific building - we only check it in the object.

Address and delivery access

The kitchen plan should take into account windows, doors, heating, ventilation and exits. If the house is used seasonally, we discuss storage and technical needs separately, rather than automatically reducing the kitchen function.

What to check at a property in Carnikava

  • Specify the exact street or house name, because the Carnikava designation does not indicate the nature of the last road section.
  • For a dwelling with an exit to the outside, mark the door leaf and the main direction of travel between the kitchen and the yard.
  • For seasonal use, describe the actual amount of equipment, utensils and food storage at different times of the year.
  • In the delivery plan, check the gate, the road surface, the unloading point and the distance where the modules have to be carried by hand.

What should be decided first in the Carnikava project

For a Carnikava home, seasonal use does not automatically mean a smaller kitchen. The maximum number of people, food stocks and how intensively the space is used during the active period should be evaluated.

Check front door and drawer conflicts before choosing fronts. Free movement to the yard is functionally more important than another full-width module.

Local Risk Check

Mode of use and access to Carnikava housing

The context of the Carnikava coast and the lower reaches of the Gauja River helps clarify the address, but is not a basis for figuring out the material requirements for a specific building. We determine them according to the construction of the room, heating, ventilation and actual use.

  • In a seasonally used home, we find out how often cooking is done, what equipment is needed and what needs to be stored between stays.
  • In a permanent residence, we give priority to daily movement, sorting, food storage and easy-to-maintain work areas.
  • Before delivery, we check the side street, the gate, the condition of the soil or pavement and the path from the entrance to the room.

In the Carnikava project, these data help to prepare not only the kitchen layout, but also the actual sequence of moving the modules to the room. Before consolidating the choices, we check together whether the assumptions about the address, use and access correspond to the actual situation.
